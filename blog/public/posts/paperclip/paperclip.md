# PaperClip – Literature Knowledge Management Tool for Academic Research

PaperClip is a literature knowledge management tool for academic researchers. It helps researchers move from passive reading to active knowledge construction by turning papers, highlights, notes, and conceptual relationships into a connected research workspace. The product is inspired by the Zettelkasten method and supported by knowledge graph technology.

## 📚 Research: Why Literature Knowledge Is Hard to Manage

Academic research is rarely a linear process. Researchers collect papers from different sources, read them at different levels of depth, take notes over a long period of time, and later need to turn scattered fragments into research questions, arguments, and topic directions.

The problem is not only storing papers. Existing tools often separate reading, note-taking, organization, and analysis into different spaces. As the number of papers grows, researchers may remember that they have read something, but forget where an idea came from, how it connects to another concept, or which papers support a certain direction.

### 👨‍🎓 User Research – Academic Researchers

The user research focused on how academic researchers collect literature, annotate papers, extract ideas, and prepare for topic selection. The findings show that researchers need a system that can preserve context during reading and later support flexible thinking across notes.

### 🔍 Technical Research – Knowledge Graph

Knowledge graph technology can transform concepts, authors, publications, and citation relationships into a structure that is readable for humans and processable by machines. This makes it suitable for literature management, because academic knowledge is naturally relational rather than only file-based.

### 📖 Competitive Analysis – Knowledge Management Tools

The competitive analysis looked at existing reading tools, note-taking tools, and knowledge management platforms. The opportunity for PaperClip is to connect the whole research flow: discovering papers, reading and annotating, organizing idea cards, and analyzing relationships.

## 🎯 Concept: From Papers to Connected Knowledge

PaperClip uses the mental model of a card box. Each highlight, note, or extracted idea can become a card. Cards can then be grouped, linked, and analyzed, so the researcher can gradually build a personal knowledge network instead of a static paper archive.

### 🔨 Product Framework

The product framework contains four core spaces: Subscription, Note, Canvas, and Analysis. Together they support the full research workflow from input to insight.

### 🔩 Function Definition

The functions are designed around a continuous loop: subscribe to relevant papers, read and annotate them, turn annotations into cards, organize cards on a canvas, and use graph analysis to discover patterns.

### 🔍 Technical Framework

The technical framework connects paper metadata, user notes, knowledge cards, and graph relationships. This allows the system to analyze both concepts mentioned in the notes and academic information linked to the papers.

![](./img/1656868367-Slide28.webp)

## 🎨 Design: Four Spaces for the Research Workflow

**Subscription**: Researchers can subscribe to papers through keywords, authors, or publications. This creates a daily reading source that is closer to their ongoing research interests.

![](./img/1656868145-Slide34.webp)

**Note**: While reading, researchers can highlight text, add comments, and extract important fragments as cards. These cards preserve the connection between the original paper and the researcher’s own thinking.

![](./img/1656868164-Slide35.webp)

**Canvas**: The canvas supports free organization of cards. Researchers can group ideas, create links, and build tree structures to explore how concepts relate to each other.

![](./img/1656868182-Slide36.webp)

**Analysis**: Researchers can select a group of cards and generate a knowledge graph. The analysis reveals relationships between concepts, papers, authors, and publications, helping researchers identify potential themes and research directions.

![](./img/1656868201-Slide37.webp)

## 💡 Summary: A Research Workspace for Thinking

PaperClip is not only a paper library. It is designed as a thinking workspace for academic research. By connecting reading, notes, cards, canvas organization, and graph analysis, it helps researchers turn accumulated literature into structured knowledge and supports the early stages of topic exploration.
