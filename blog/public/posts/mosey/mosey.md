# MOSEY – 2030 Driving Experience Design

**_Mosey is a future driving experience design based on human-machine symbiosis._**

In 2030, autonomous driving may sit between Level 3 and Level 4. The vehicle can handle more driving tasks, but the driver still cannot fully leave the driving context. This transitional stage creates a new challenge: the boundary between human control and machine control becomes blurred, and trust can easily weaken when the driver no longer understands what the vehicle is doing or why.

MOSEY explores a warmer human-vehicle relationship for this in-between moment. Instead of treating the car as either a passive tool or a fully independent machine, the project imagines the vehicle as a partner that communicates its state, intention, and limits in a way the driver can gradually understand.

## Design Direction

The goal is to build a sense of mutual understanding. The driver should know when the car is confident, when it needs attention, and when control may need to shift back. At the same time, the system should learn from the driver's habits and respond in a manner that feels consistent and predictable.

## Summary

MOSEY frames future driving experience as a relationship design problem. As automation increases, the key question is not only how capable the system is, but how clearly it can share responsibility with the person inside the vehicle.
