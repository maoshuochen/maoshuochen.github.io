# Cubes of Growth – Footbridge design

Cubes of Growth is a footbridge concept that explores how a piece of infrastructure can feel alive inside a natural landscape. The design uses branching white structures around the bridge deck, creating an experience that sits between architecture, sculpture, and a walking path.

## Concept

The bridge is imagined as more than a linear connection across water. It becomes a spatial sequence where people move through a field of growing forms. The white branching elements echo organic structures such as roots, trunks, and ice-covered trees, while the warm light along the deck gives the path a clear direction and a sense of safety.

## Experience

From a distance, the bridge reads as a soft, almost natural silhouette rather than a conventional engineered object. As visitors approach, the structure frames views of the water and surrounding trees. The contrast between the misty environment, the pale branching forms, and the illuminated walkway creates a quiet, immersive atmosphere.

## Summary

The project treats the footbridge as a small public experience rather than only a crossing. By combining circulation, light, and organic structural language, Cubes of Growth proposes a bridge that can become a memorable place within the landscape.
